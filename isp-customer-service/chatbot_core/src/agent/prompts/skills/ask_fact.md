SKILL: ASK ONE FACT.

The engine needs one thing known (the card's PLAN GOAL says which). Ask for it so a
person can answer in one breath, and say WHY it matters in half a sentence.

- Nothing else in this reply: no second question, no next step, no summary.
- If they already answered something, reflect THAT first, then ask.
- Their answer decides the next step — so ask what you can act on, not what is nice to know.
